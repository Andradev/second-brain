# Adapta, MyHub ou Inner AI: Qual a Melhor?

Source path: `01-raw/videos/processed/Adapta, MyHub ou Inner AI Qual a Melhor A Verdade Imparcial e Sem Marketing.md`

Processed date: 2026-05-11

## Short Summary

This source evaluates platforms such as Adapta, MyHub, and Inner AI that market access to multiple AI models under a single subscription. The main argument is that their durable value is education and packaging, not proprietary AI infrastructure: the chat UI is an intermediary over third-party model APIs, and the business works because manual users consume far less API value than the subscription price.

## Key Claims Or Observations

- The platforms are characterized as education businesses with a model-access interface attached.
- The source argues that "all AI models for one subscription" works because the provider earns the spread between subscription revenue and API usage cost.
- The absence of API access is presented as a business-model necessity: automation could consume enough tokens to break the unlimited-plan economics.
- Alternatives named include OpenRouter-style pay-per-use access, T3 Chat, LLM Chat, and direct Gemini subscriptions.
- The recommended decision rule is to subscribe for the courses and community if those are valuable, not mainly for access to AI models.

## Contradictions, Tensions, Or Updates

- The source challenges marketing that frames these tools as technology platforms, arguing their defensible value is educational content and distribution.
- It adds a pricing/economics lens to the wiki's broader interest in AI tooling: usability and packaging can be valuable even when the underlying model access is commoditized.

## Open Questions

- The assessment is opinionated and may need corroboration from current pricing, product limits, and actual course quality before purchase decisions.
- It is unclear whether the user's interest is tool access, education, automation API usage, or all three.
