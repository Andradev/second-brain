Processed date: 2026-05-11

## Short Summary

Matheus Battisti explains Karpathy's LLM Wiki method for using Obsidian with Claude Code. The video demonstrates taking raw Markdown sources, asking the coding agent to compile them into a wiki, and then querying the resulting knowledge base for specific facts, cross-source connections, and content ideas.

## Key Claims Or Observations

- The method keeps sources in the `raw/` source layer and compiles them into `wiki/` pages that can be navigated in Obsidian or queried through an LLM.
- Obsidian Web Clipper is recommended for turning web pages and YouTube transcripts into Markdown.
- The wiki can create source, entity, concept, model, overview, and synthesis pages depending on the schema.
- The graph view becomes useful because the LLM creates links among concepts and entities.
- The most valuable use is not just reading pages, but asking the LLM for synthesis and hidden connections across the saved corpus.
- The system supports content creation by suggesting topics from the user's accumulated knowledge.
