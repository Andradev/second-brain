# AI Second Brain

An AI second brain is a personal knowledge system where saved notes, sources, transcripts, journal entries, and relationship context can be organized and queried with AI assistance.

## Current Synthesis

The sources describe two levels of second brain. The first is a manual Obsidian habit: capture notes, link ideas, and review regularly. The second is an AI-maintained layer where agents summarize raw sources, extract entities and concepts, cross-link pages, and answer future questions from the accumulated wiki.

The strongest version combines storage, synthesis, and action. [[03-wiki/sources/build-a-second-brain-that-remembers-everything|Matt Wolfe]] extends the wiki into journal and CRM workflows, while [[03-wiki/sources/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]] gives the minimal architecture for making the knowledge layer compound over time.
