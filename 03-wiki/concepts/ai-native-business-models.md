# AI-Native Business Models

AI-native business models use AI as a core delivery layer, not just as a feature added to an existing product or service.

## Current Synthesis

[[03-wiki/sources/6-negocios-de-ia-que-ainda-nao-tem-concorrencia|6 Negocios de IA que Ainda Nao Tem Concorrencia]] gives the broadest taxonomy: AI-native services, company brains, AI-native hedge funds, SaaS challengers, software for agents, and AI-native agencies. The shared mechanism is that AI lowers the cost of production and shifts value toward choosing the right market, packaging outcomes, and owning context.

[[03-wiki/sources/adapta-myhub-ou-inner-ai-qual-a-melhor|Adapta, MyHub ou Inner AI]] adds a business-model caution: some AI products are distribution and education plays over commodity model APIs, so value should be judged by outcome, teaching, and workflow fit rather than vague "AI access."
