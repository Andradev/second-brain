# Company Brain

A company brain is a centralized, AI-usable system of record for an organization's context, processes, prompts, manuals, skills, and operating knowledge.

## Current Synthesis

The company brain is the organizational version of the personal [[wiki/concepts/ai-second-brain|AI Second Brain]]. Instead of storing only personal interests, it collects the context agents need to work inside a company: reimbursement rules, hiring processes, security incident procedures, writing guidelines, customer context, and tool instructions.

The source frames it as a system of record with switching costs: once a company's agent manuals and workflow context live there, replacing it becomes difficult.
