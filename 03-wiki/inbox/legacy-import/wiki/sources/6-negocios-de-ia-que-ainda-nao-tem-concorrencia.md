# 6 Negocios de IA que Ainda Nao Tem Concorrencia

Source path: `raw/processed/6 Negócios de IA que Ainda Não Têm Concorrência.md`

Processed date: 2026-05-11

## Short Summary

Deborah Folloni curates AI business opportunities inspired partly by Y Combinator requests for startups and current market shifts. The source argues that the bottleneck has moved from building software to choosing the right opportunity, because AI lowers implementation cost and changes whether companies buy software, build internally, or buy outcome-oriented services.

## Key Claims Or Observations

- AI-native services may be more attractive than SaaS because the seller captures service-market budgets while using AI to improve margins.
- A [[wiki/concepts/company-brain|company brain]] is framed as a system of record for organizational context, manuals, prompts, skills, and connectors that agents can use on demand.
- AI-native hedge fund tooling, financial research agents, and trading frameworks are presented as an emerging opportunity area.
- SaaS challengers can attack incumbents through lower-cost clones, AI-native redesigns, or open-source products monetized through hosting and services.
- [[wiki/concepts/agent-friendly-software|Agent-friendly software]] should expose documentation, APIs, CLIs, MCPs, and skills rather than only human-oriented UIs.
- AI-native agencies can sell finished outcomes with software-like margins because AI reduces the labor needed to deliver creative and operational work.
