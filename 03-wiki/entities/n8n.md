# n8n

Workflow automation platform used in the WhatsApp AI agent and CRM tutorial.

## Current Synthesis

n8n is used to receive WhatsApp webhook events, process lead data, call an AI model, write to Supabase, and send responses back through Z-API. In the source, n8n is the automation backbone behind the visible CRM.
