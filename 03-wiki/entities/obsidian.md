# Obsidian

Local-first Markdown note app used as the visible workspace for this vault and for several second-brain workflows in the sources.

## Current Synthesis

Obsidian is valued because notes are local Markdown files, links are first-class, graph view reveals connections, and Web Clipper can capture articles and YouTube transcripts. In the LLM Wiki pattern, Obsidian is the human-facing frontend while the LLM edits the wiki files.
