# Overview

This wiki currently focuses on AI-augmented knowledge work and AI-assisted building. The first ingested batch combines the original [[wiki/concepts/llm-wiki-pattern|LLM Wiki Pattern]], Obsidian second-brain workflows, agentic coding methods, AI CRM builds, design-system-assisted UI generation, model aggregator economics, and AI-native business opportunities.

The strongest throughline is context. Personal knowledge bases, company brains, software specs, design systems, CRM histories, and agent-friendly tools all solve the same problem from different angles: AI becomes more useful when it has structured, durable, relevant context rather than loose prompts.

## Major Topic Areas

- [[wiki/concepts/ai-second-brain|AI Second Brain]] and [[wiki/concepts/llm-wiki-pattern|LLM Wiki Pattern]] - how this vault turns raw sources into a maintained knowledge layer.
- [[wiki/concepts/agentic-coding-workflow|Agentic Coding Workflow]] and [[wiki/concepts/spec-driven-agentic-development|Spec-Driven Agentic Development]] - how to build software with AI agents without losing architecture, state, or consistency.
- [[wiki/concepts/ai-agent-crm|AI Agent CRM]] - how AI conversations can become durable customer or relationship memory.
- [[wiki/concepts/ai-native-business-models|AI-Native Business Models]] and [[wiki/concepts/company-brain|Company Brain]] - how AI changes business opportunities and organizational context systems.
- [[wiki/concepts/design-systems-for-ai-codegen|Design Systems For AI Codegen]] and [[wiki/concepts/model-aggregator-platforms|Model Aggregator Platforms]] - practical tooling patterns and buying decisions.
